Scientific Claim Checker

Checks a health or science claim against 5,183 research abstracts and says whether the papers support it, refute it, or don't say — with citations.

  • 75% correct verdicts on 300 test claims
  • right paper in top 10 for 86% of claims
  • 0.12 s search
  • $0 to run
How do the methods differ?
  • Keywords only (BM25), right paper in the top 10 for 0.819 of claims: matches exact words, great for drug and gene names, misses synonyms.
  • Meaning only (embeddings, bge-small), 0.836: matches meaning and handles synonyms, but can blur precise terms.
  • Hybrid, 0.855 (default): runs both and merges the rankings (Reciprocal Rank Fusion), so each catches what the other misses.

Measured on 300 SciFact test claims. Verdicts always use hybrid search, the measured setting; this choice only changes the paper list.
